Privacy Policy

SoundPilot AI · Effective November 9, 2026

English한국어Support / 지원

Summary

SoundPilot AI does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data that stays on your iPad

  • Console settings you save (venue profiles, service order, presets, snapshots, logs) are stored only on your iPad.
  • Your Claude API key, if you add one, is stored only in your iPad’s Keychain.

Local network

The app connects directly to your mixing console on your local network to read and change console settings. This traffic never leaves your network.

Microphone

The optional room analysis uses the iPad microphone to measure reverb time and find feedback frequencies. Audio is analysed in memory in real time and is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Optional AI features (Anthropic)

If you add your own Claude API key and use the AI chat or AI consulting report, the text you type and a summary of the console state (channel names, levels and settings) are sent directly from your iPad to Anthropic’s API to generate a response. This is processed under Anthropic’s terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We do not receive or store this data. Without an API key, nothing is sent.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no personal information.

아이패드에만 저장되는 정보

AI 기능(선택, Anthropic)

본인의 Claude API 키를 넣고 AI 채팅이나 AI 컨설팅 보고서를 쓰면, 입력한 문장과 콘솔 상태 요약(채널 이름·레벨·설정)이 아이패드에서 Anthropic API로 직접 전송되어 답변을 만듭니다. 이 정보는 Anthropic의 약관과 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 처리되며, 저희는 받거나 저장하지 않습니다. API 키가 없으면 아무것도 전송되지 않습니다.
